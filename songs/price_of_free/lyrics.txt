THE PRICE OF FREE

[Intro — Distant notification sounds, vinyl static, low piano, heartbeat kick]

[Male voice, quiet and conversational]

Funny thing about a cage...

If you make it comfortable enough...

People start decorating it.

[Beat drops — Heavy distorted 808, razor-sharp snare, chopped cello loop]

[Verse 1 — Conscious rap, intricate internal rhymes, laid-back precision]

Six in the morning, alarm screaming like a siren,
Screen full of violence, somebody selling silence.
Thumb on the glass, another tragedy to scroll,
A million little windows but no windows to the soul.

Coffee in a cup with a motivational phrase,
"Live your best life" on a minimum wage.
Got a thousand notifications, not a single real connection,
Got a camera in my pocket teaching me to crave attention.

Everybody got a platform, everybody got a stance,
Everybody got opinions that they never had a chance
To examine, but they're adamant, attacking for the camera,
While the algorithm calculates the damage and the stamina.

We don't talk, we trade reactions, that's a chemical transaction,
Anger gets engagement, so they manufacture factions.
I was looking for the truth, got a mirror full of ads,
Now my childhood's in a cloud and my future's in a bag.

[Pre-Chorus — Drums strip away, aching cello melody, half-sung male vocals]

We built ourselves a kingdom out of things we couldn't keep,
Bought a thousand ways to wake up, still forgot how to sleep.

[Chorus — Catchy melodic male rap hook, huge drums, stacked harmonies]

They put a price on free,
Put a lock on the key,
Sold me back my own reflection,
Called it individuality.

Got the whole world in my hand,
But it's slipping through my fingers,
If the cage is made of gold,
Does that make the chains feel different?

[Post-Chorus — Hard 808 drop, chopped vocal samples]

Free, free — tell me what it means.
Everybody selling freedom,
Everybody buying dreams.

[Verse 2 — Beat switch, syncopated trap drums, faster rhythmic flow]

Met a man on the corner with a cardboard constitution,
Said he'd worked his whole life, now he's begging for a solution.
Across the street a billboard said, "Your happiness awaits,"
With a luxury apartment and a digit on the rates.

There's a child in the classroom learning how to pass a test,
Not to question who's deciding what intelligence is best.
There's a mother working doubles, she ain't seen her son in days,
But her phone congratulated her for reaching thirty days.

And I laughed — till I caught myself recording,
Trying to turn somebody's pain into a story worth rewarding.
Had a caption, had a filter, had a message about compassion,
Then I checked to see the numbers, and I hated my reaction.

See, I thought I was the rebel, I was different, I was awake,
But I monetized my outrage and repackaged my mistakes.
I was yelling at the system through a system-owned device,
And the louder that I shouted, man, the higher went the price.

[Pre-Chorus — Strings swell, snare roll accelerates]

Maybe freedom isn't something that somebody's gonna sell,
Maybe knowing there's a door means you gotta walk yourself.

[Chorus — Full drums, cinematic strings, powerful vocal performance]

They put a price on free,
Put a lock on the key,
Sold me back my own reflection,
Called it individuality.

Got the whole world in my hand,
But it's slipping through my fingers,
If the cage is made of gold,
Does that make the chains feel different?

[Bridge — Abrupt silence, heartbeat kick, intimate spoken rap]

I blamed the politicians.
I blamed the billionaires.
I blamed the talking heads
And everybody sitting in their chairs.

But every time I fed the fire,
Every time I chose a side,
Every time I shared a headline
That I never verified...

I was turning every gear
In the machine I criticized.

[Beat Switch — Half-time orchestral trap, distorted cello, thunderous kick]

[Verse 3 — Intensifying flow, controlled anger, double-time bursts]

So I put the phone down, heard the city breathe,
Saw a stranger on the sidewalk looking just as lost as me.
Didn't ask him what he voted, didn't ask him what he earned,
Didn't tell him what to think, just asked him what he'd learned.

Funny how a conversation doesn't need a corporation,
Doesn't need a recommendation or a monetized sensation.
And the world didn't change, no miraculous transition,
Just a moment where a human saw a human, not a position.

Maybe that's the revolution, not a hashtag, not a trend,
Not a digital crusade against a manufactured enemy.
Not a savior in a suit, not another perfect plan,
Just the courage to be wrong and the discipline to understand.

[Final Chorus — Epic male vocal harmonies, orchestral crescendo, massive drums]

They put a price on free,
But they couldn't buy the key.
I don't need another mirror
To decide what's real to me.

Got the whole world in my hand,
So I'm learning when to leave it.
If the cage is made of gold,
I can still refuse to need it.

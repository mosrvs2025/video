# Experience 1: storylines

**The one thing:** grief is hearing someone everywhere in a room that no longer holds them. One object carries it all: the empty chair by the window.

**What the song is** (measured, not guessed): 3:37, ≈85 BPM. 0:00–0:44 wordless intro that builds; 0:44 verse 1; 1:00 chorus ("echoes in my head"); 1:32 verse 2 (sparse); 1:57–2:19 instrumental build to a hit at 2:19, then a hollow; 2:35 near-silence (rms 0.02); 2:40 "the rooms have changed their names"; 2:47 final chorus; 3:04 wordless harmonies; 3:20–3:27 near-silence, then a whispered "and… nothing." (3:26); 3:28–3:34 a last surge; cut to silence at 3:34.5.

## A. The Room (picked)
**World:** one decaying plaster room with a tall window (the cover art's room). **Device:** the sound *is* the light: the window light is 32 shafts, one per frequency band, so you literally see every sound. A presence made of dust motes sits in the chair; sung phrases send echo ripples across the floor; breath fogs the glass. **Form:** one continuous take; the only cuts are velocity-matched door-jamb wipes through rooms that keep changing while the chair stays.
First two seconds: dim room, the chair in cold window light, a handful of motes catching it.
Beats: wake (intro) → build (light grows) → the presence assembles (verse 1) → echoes (chorus) → it vanishes, the chair is empty (74 s) → fog, the little things on the sill (verse 2) → the rooms change (bridge, door wipes) → the hit, then hollow → embers with no flame → final chorus, the presence frays to air → wordless harmonies: the chair multiplies into echoes → near-silence, "nothing." in the fog → last surge of light → silence.
Sound: the song only. Nothing is added.

## B. The Instrument (not built)
Abstract: the song as a 3-D sculpture of rings, ribbons and a waveform tunnel; the camera flies through it. Maximum "see the sounds", but no story and the "spectrum as the main picture" tell from the music-video rules.

## C. The Little Things (not built)
A tabletop of objects (cup, key, ring, letter), each lit and animated on its line, match-cutting between them (macro 3-D). Warm and intimate, but the song's biggest moments (the hit at 2:19, the silences, the surge) have no place to land.

## Why A
It gives the loudest and quietest moments somewhere to happen (a whiteout, a held breath, a vanishing), it has one hero object, and the sound-made-visible idea is built into the world instead of laid on top.

# Style brief — "The Price of Free" (v3 look: **The Aviary**)

**What we are NOT repeating.** Manufactured Ease = cream/red/ink poster plates, sunburst backdrops, glitch cuts, shouty
poster type. Attempt 1 of this song = navy/steel/gold poster plates with the same cuts. Everything below changes the
*grammar*, not just the colours.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Night aubergine `#150a22` (ground) · saffron-brass `#f0b43c` (the cage, everything "gilded") · notification rose `#ff4f8b` (the only hot colour: badges, ads, alarms, the price tag) · lilac bone `#eadff6` (type + paper). Green is reserved for the YOU key (`#00ff00`), so nothing else in the picture is green. |
| **Layout grammar** | A **shadow-box diorama**, not a poster: 4–5 paper-cut layers with real parallax and one lantern light that casts soft drop-shadows. The 9:16 frame is *the inside of a huge birdcage* — thin brass bars run top-to-bottom in front of everything, the diorama sits in the upper ~64 %, lyrics sit in a **brass-plate caption band** in the lower third (never over the art). No full-frame text plates, no sunbursts. |
| **Motion language** | **One continuous camera, never a hard cut.** Slow dolly/tilt with parallax; the kick makes the cage bars *flex* (they bow inward and spring back) and the hanging cage swing; scene changes are the camera pushing *through* the bars/door into the next diorama (bar-sweep wipe). Lyrics land like a **letterpress stamp** (settle from 106 % with a slight squash and a soft ink bleed), fast bars as 2–3-word chunks that slide on a rail 70 ms before the vocal. No glitch, no RGB-split, no screen shake. |
| **Recurring image** | **The gilded birdcage**, and it has an arc: V1 it is decorated and comfortable (ribbons, fairy lights, a phone as the perch) → Chorus 1 locked, ornate, tag hanging off it ("PRICE: FREE") → V2 the bars are made of billboards, price tags, wifi bars → Chorus 2 hairline cracks → Bridge the door appears → V3 the door is ajar and the city shows through → Final Chorus the door is open and the bird is gone. Repeated choruses are the *same cage in a later state*, so they never look identical. |
| **Typography** | **Fraunces** (soft-serif, variable weight) for lyrics — Black Italic for the stamped key word, Regular for the rest — set on the brass plate in lilac bone, key word in rose. **DM Mono** for small data-ish labels (timestamps, "NOTIFICATION", prices). Large, calm, high-contrast; it should feel like an engraved auction catalogue rather than a poster. |

**Per-line scenes (examples)** — "alarm like a siren": the cage bell-jar, alarm bell ringing, bars flex on every beat ·
"windows to the soul": a wall of glowing windows inside the cage, the one at the centre is a mirror · "coffee … minimum
wage": a paper cup, motivational sleeve text, a coin counter · "kingdom": a paper-crown on a stack of boxes · "price on
free": a hanging rose price tag swinging from the cage · "lock on the key": big brass padlock, key out of reach ·
"billboard": a paper billboard with a tiny luxury flat · "caught myself recording": a phone-lens iris closes over the
cage · "turning every gear": gear train, bars as spokes · "put the phone down … city breathe": the cage door opens, the
city skyline breathes (scales with the low band).

**YOU slots.** 8 fixed 3:4 windows (480×640 in the 720×1280 design space = 720×960 px in the 1080×1920 render), each
inside an ornate brass frame so they read as "a portrait hung in the cage" — the diorama behind goes to a quiet
ambient state for those 4–8 s.

How the lyric timing was made

Vocals isolated from your Suno export, then forced-aligned to your exact lyrics with Montreal Forced Aligner (per-line refinement). Fast bars are shown as 2–3 word chunks that appear 70 ms before the vocal onset. Lines are colour-coded by alignment confidence.

  • Not Demucs / WhisperX: those model weights were unreachable in the build sandbox.
  • Word boundaries inside fast bars can be off by roughly 30–80 ms; line starts are the most reliable.
  • Two lines were placed by hand (see timing report).
